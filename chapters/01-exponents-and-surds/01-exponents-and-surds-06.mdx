**Problem:**

**Problem:**

**Problem:**
\(27(4^x) = (64)3^x\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}

                                                \frac{27}{64} &= \frac{3^x}{4^x} \\
                                                \frac{3^3}{4^3} &= \left( \frac{3}{4} \right)^x \\
                                                \left( \frac{3}{4} \right)^3 &= \left( \frac{3}{4} \right)^x \\

**Problem:**
\(\sqrt{2x - 5} = 2 - x\)

**Problem:**
\(2x^{\frac{2}{3}} + 3x^{\frac{1}{3}} - 2 = 0\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
                                                2x^{\frac{2}{3}} + 3x^{\frac{1}{3}} - 2 &= 0 \\
                                                \left( 2x^{\frac{1}{3}} - 1 \right) \left( x^{\frac{1}{3}} + 2 \right)
                                                &= 0 \\
                                                \therefore 2x^{\frac{1}{3}} - 1 &= 0 \\
                                                2x^{\frac{1}{3}} &= 1 \\
                                                x^{\frac{1}{3}} &= \frac{1}{2} \\
                                                \left( x^{\frac{1}{3}} \right)^3 &= \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^3 \\
                                                \therefore x &= \frac{1}{8} \\
                                                \text{ or } & \\

                                                \left( x^{\frac{1}{3}} \right)^3 &= \left( -2 \right)^3 \\

**Problem:**
Show that \(\sqrt{\dfrac{3^{x+1} - 3^x}{3^{x-1}} + 3}\) is equal to
                                                        \(\text{3}\)

**Solution:**
\begin{align*}
                                                \text{LHS } &= \sqrt{\dfrac{3^{x+1} - 3^x}{3^{x-1}} + 3} \\
                                                &= \sqrt{\dfrac{3^{x+1}}{3^{x-1}} - \dfrac{3^{x}}{3^{x-1}}+ 3} \\
                                                &= \sqrt{3^{x+1-x+1} - 3^{x-x+1} + 3} \\
                                                &= \sqrt{3^{2} - 3^{1} + 3} \\
                                                &= \sqrt{3^{2}} \\
                                                &= 3 \\
                                                &= \text{RHS }
                                                \end{align*}

**Problem:**
