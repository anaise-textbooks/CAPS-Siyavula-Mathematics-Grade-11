## 2.10 Summary (EMBFZ)

- Zero product law: if \(a \times b = 0\), then \(a = 0\) and/or \(b=0\).
- Quadratic formula: \(x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\).
- Discriminant: \(\Delta = b^2 - 4ac\).
  | Nature of roots | Discriminant |
  | --- | --- |
  | Roots are non-real | \(\Delta <0\) |
  | Roots are real and equal | \(\Delta = 0\) |
  | Roots are real and unequal: Rational roots Irrational roots | \(\Delta > 0\) \(Δ =\) squared rational number \(Δ =\) not squared rational number |
